# R1 — the extended year (2026), both worlds

R0 froze the incident year (2025). R1 freezes what the demo says about **2026**, which exists only
once the data is extended (`data/extend/`, LR-P4, contracts C-8). Every figure here is a function of
the 2024 template plus the deterministic jitter, so a world extended to the same date reproduces it
exactly.

**Extended to 2026-10-31** — the facts run to the end of that week, **2026-11-02** (sales; inventory
to 2026-10-30). January 2026 mixes original rows (store/web to 01-02, marketplace to 01-08) with
extended ones; every later month is extended. Source: `results-{us,cz}/r14_extended_months.csv` plus
the queries quoted below.

> **Provenance (2026-10-10).** Computed on a throwaway local Postgres holding the same pipeline as
> the fixture — the 2026-07-09 re-dated US dump + `localize-cz` 00/02/03 + seeds S1–S3 + the DC
> names — because the dsk fixture was unreachable that day. That copy reproduces the committed
> `results-{us,cz}/r01_channel_year.csv` 2024 and 2025 revenue to the unit, in both worlds, and the
> extension reads only those years. **G3 (Bora):** after `just extend-data cz 2026-10-31` on the
> hartland cluster, re-run `RECON_ONLY=r14_extended_months ./run-recon.sh hartland hartland_cz hartland-pg-1 hartland_cz_readonly`
> and diff against `results-cz/r14_extended_months.csv`. Identical = R1 holds; any difference = the
> cluster's data is not the fixture's, and R1 is re-read from the cluster.

## Refreshing R1 after a prolong

1. `just extend-data <us|cz> <until>` on the cluster (and the other world if it is shown).
2. `RECON_ONLY=r14_extended_months data/recon/run-recon.sh <ctx> hartland_<w> hartland-pg-1 hartland_<w>_readonly`,
   then `mv data/recon/results/r14_extended_months.csv data/recon/results-<w>/`.
3. Re-read the tables above from it (the per-DC rows: the query under "Marketplace per DC" in the
   LR-P4 PR, or `GROUP BY w_warehouse_name` over `catalog_sales` for the new month); commit.
4. Re-read any figure the demo script quotes from R1.
